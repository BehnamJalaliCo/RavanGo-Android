2) Original RavanGo artwork (no third-party art)

- Lens sprites (sunglasses, crown, cat ears/nose/whiskers, stars, sparkles, hearts, rainbow) are drawn
  procedurally at runtime with android.graphics (effects/SpriteAtlas.kt).
- Face paint (freckles, blush, shimmer) and makeup textures are generated procedurally in the face-mesh UV space
  (effects/FacePaint.kt, makeup/MakeupAtlas.kt).
- Live colour filters are 3D LUTs computed procedurally (effects/LutGenerator.kt).
